FAQ

Your Home Care Questions Answered

Clear answers to common questions about long-term care insurance and family protection.

Long-Term Care insurance helps pay for extended care services required when someone is unable to perform basic activities of daily living (bathing, dressing, eating, transferring) or suffers from cognitive impairment like Alzheimer’s or dementia. Regular health insurance and Medicare typically do not cover custodial or ongoing personal care.

CLTC stands for Certified in Long-Term Care. It is the premier, multidisciplinary credential in extended care planning. It confirms that Ellen Atkins has completed rigorous training in the legal, financial, and emotional aspects of long-term care to best advise families objectively.

Yes, absolutely! Modern comprehensive LTC policies are specifically designed to prioritize in-home care, allowing you or your loved ones to receive high-quality assistance, homemaker services, and skilled nursing while remaining in the comfort of your home.

The sweet spot for exploring long-term care insurance is typically in your 50s to early 60s. During this window, you are most likely to qualify medically for preferred health discounts, locking in substantially lower annual premiums for life.

Our initial consultation is completely complimentary and educational. We review your family goals, assess existing benefits, and answer questions with zero pressure or obligation. We can meet in person in Greensboro, NC, by phone, or virtually via video conference.
